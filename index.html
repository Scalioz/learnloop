How it works

From lesson to "I've got this" in three steps.

1

Add your lesson

Upload a PDF, snap a photo, paste notes, add a YouTube transcript, record your class or type a topic.

2

LearnLoop builds your set

In about a minute you get notes, flashcards, a quiz and a practice test, all from your own material.

3

Practise and loop back

Test yourself, see what's weak, and revise those parts again until everything is mastered.

Why it works

Built on how memory really works.

1

Active recall

Testing yourself makes your brain pull the answer out, which locks it in far better than reading it again.

2

Spaced repetition

Revising after a gap of a few days fights the forgetting curve. LearnLoop tells you which sets are due.

3

Learn from mistakes

Every wrong answer comes with a reason, so the same mistake doesn't happen twice.

FAQ

Questions students ask.

Is LearnLoop free?

Yes. You can create study sets and use every feature without paying.

Do I need to sign up?

No account is needed. Your study sets and progress are saved on your own phone or laptop, in the browser you use.

Which boards does it support?

CBSE and ICSE for Class 9 and 10, and Cambridge IGCSE. Choose your board once, and every note, quiz and test follows your syllabus and exam style. You can change it any time.

Which subjects does it work for?

Any subject with something to read or listen to: Science, Social Science, English, Maths theory, Hindi and more. Photo Solve helps with step-by-step maths sums.

Is the AI always right?

It is accurate most of the time, but AI can make small mistakes. Always check important facts and answers with your textbook or teacher.

Does voice recording work on my phone?

Voice recording and Listen Mode work best in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge, on a computer or an Android phone. On other browsers you can still type or paste.

Can it read my handwritten notes?

Yes, if the photo is clear and well lit. Neat handwriting works best.
